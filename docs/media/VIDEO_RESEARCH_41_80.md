## Verification

For every proposed or conditional video, YouTube oEmbed returned HTTP 200, and the current YouTube watch-page player response returned status `OK` and `playableInEmbed: true`. Titles/channel names come from oEmbed. Exercise identity was checked against narration/descriptions where available, clinic source pages where useful, and sampled frames from YouTube's storyboard images. This is frame-sampled visual verification, not a claim that every second of each video was watched. Some videos include other exercises; match notes identify which segment applies. Availability and regional restrictions can change.

Bare direct embed navigation can produce YouTube Error 153 when no Referer is sent. That is a player configuration issue, not evidence the publisher disabled embedding. The app must send an appropriate HTTP Referer. Do not set no-referrer on the iframe or page.

## Content boundaries

This mapping verifies demonstrations, not the safety or correctness of every existing Arabic cue, muscle attribution, clinical claim or dosage in the repository. In particular, claims of isolated VMO training and the identity of soleus push-ups versus generic calf raises deserve clinical review. No dosage, outcome claim, or recommendation in a source video is adopted by this report.

Entry 67 remains conditional: its selected Hinge Health video is a flat-floor seated calf raise, while the current repository cue asks to lower below a step. Entries 55 and 64 should use the app's honest unavailable-video state, not fabricated IDs or adjacent exercise variants.
